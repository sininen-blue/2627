What is the primary reason logic gates are never sold as individual components?

- Gates are fabricated together on a single piece of silicon
- Gates are too small for machines to handle alone
- Gates need liquid cooling to operate reliably

What purpose do the pins extending from an IC package serve?

- They carry input, output, and power connections
- They protect the silicon die from physical damage
- They amplify signals before they leave the chip
- They store data while the chip is powered off

How do PGA and LGA packages differ in the way they connect to a socket?

- PGA has pins on the chip while LGA has pins in the socket
- PGA uses flat contact pads while LGA uses protruding pins
- PGA plugs into the socket while LGA clips around the socket
- PGA suits small ICs while LGA suits large CPUs
